A Bridge Road Institution

Anchored Here for 25 Years

For a quarter century, The Wheelhouse has been the go-to lunch counter at the Bridge Road Shops. Every morning starts the same way: chicken salad and egg salad made from scratch, soup simmering for the day, and the bakery case filling up with crumbcake, babka, turnovers and cinnamon rolls.

No shortcuts, no freezer bags — just honest, homemade food served fast and friendly.
